OUR SERVICES

Consulting and
custom software.

Whether you are planning a new platform or improving an existing system, we help define the requirements and develop the software around them.

01

Technology consulting

Before development begins, we help you decide what the system needs to do. We review existing workflows, define requirements and assess the technology choices against the business problem.

02

E-commerce development

We develop commerce platforms around how your business sells: how products are organised, how buyers find them and how enquiries or orders are handled. The scope follows your sales process.

03

Logistics software

Software for the coordination behind a shipment: vehicle availability, capacity, assignments and progress updates. We design the flow of information between transporters, brokers, shippers and the teams managing the work.

04

IoT & telematics

Consulting and software development for systems that use data from vehicles and connected devices. We help define what to measure, how to bring the data together and how operators should see and use it.
