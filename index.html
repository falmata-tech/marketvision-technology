OUR DIRECTION

Trade depends
on information.

A supplier can be ready to sell yet difficult to find. A transporter can have available space without the right shipper knowing. Useful information needs to reach the people who can act on it.

Our vision

Our aim is to connect African markets and improve visibility—making businesses easier to find and giving them a clearer view of their operations.

Our mission

Build commerce platforms, logistics systems and tools that turn vehicle and device data into useful information.
