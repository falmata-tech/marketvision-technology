Independent platforms.
A connected vision.

A producer needs to reach buyers. A logistics provider needs to reach customers. A transport team needs to make available capacity visible. We build platforms around these practical connections.

MarketVision owns, develops and operates each platform. Each works on its own, with a shared purpose: putting useful information and commercial relationships within reach.

Our platforms.

Three focused platforms. Use what fits your business.

01 / 03

Discover what Africa makes.

AfricMade gives farms, makers and manufacturers a digital showroom for products, business details and location. Buyers can discover suppliers and send enquiries for a single item or a larger order, then agree availability and terms directly.

  • Location search
  • Product collections
  • Enquiry lists
Visit AfricMade (opens in a new tab)
02 / 03

Logistics conversations across platforms.

CargoChatter brings provider profiles and shipment conversations into websites and apps. Hosts choose which providers appear; buyers and sellers discuss shipments with them. Providers manage their profiles, assign enquiries to teammates and respond across participating sites, keeping control of their quotes and service.

  • Website & app widgets
  • Provider selection
  • Team enquiry inbox
Visit CargoChatter (opens in a new tab)
03 / 03

See capacity. Coordinate transport.

Loadgistic helps transporters manage vehicles and share capacity and routes publicly or with selected customers. Shippers and brokers can review available space and build their transporter network. Private updates keep the people involved in agreed shipments informed.

  • Fleet management
  • Capacity sharing
  • Private shipment updates
Visit Loadgistic (opens in a new tab)
