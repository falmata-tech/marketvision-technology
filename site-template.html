Independent platforms.
A connected vision.

Finding a supplier, making a sale and arranging the movement of goods are connected parts of trade, yet often happen through separate channels. MarketVision develops distinct technology platforms around these needs: business discovery, logistics conversations accessible across participating websites and apps, and visibility into transport capacity.

Our platforms.

01 / 03

Discover what Africa makes.

AfricMade brings African farms, manufacturers and independent makers into a marketplace built around the supplier. Each showroom combines product collections with business and location details, giving buyers a fuller picture of who they are buying from.

  • Browse by location
  • Product collections
  • Enquiry lists

02 / 03

The logistics conversation layer for African commerce.

CargoChatter connects two sides of a growing network. E-commerce stores, B2B marketplaces and business apps can embed its widget to give buyers and sellers access to selected freight brokers, transport operators, logistics companies and express parcel providers where transactions happen. Providers, in turn, can gain visibility and shipment enquiries across participating platforms—not just through their own websites.

  • Embedded logistics widget
  • Curated provider network
  • Direct shipment enquiries
Visit CargoChatter (opens in a new tab)
03 / 03

Visibility across your transport network.

Loadgistic makes vehicle availability and routes visible across transport networks. Transporters decide what to share publicly and what to make available only to selected customers, while shippers and brokers can review capacity and keep track of providers they work with.

  • Vehicle capacity
  • Transporter networks
  • Private shipment updates
