THE COMPANY

A portfolio shaped
by the needs of trade.

Choosing a supplier, arranging delivery and finding suitable transport each require different information and relationships. MarketVision develops focused platforms around these needs, bringing commerce, logistics communication and transport visibility into a common portfolio.

As the parent company, we guide the direction, development and ongoing operation of each platform. Each has a distinct purpose, with room to grow around the businesses and customers it serves.

OUR PORTFOLIO

Our platforms.

Three platforms, serving distinct needs across commerce and logistics.

BUSINESS DISCOVERY01 / 03
Illustration

Discover what Africa makes.

AfricMade brings African farms, manufacturers and independent makers into a marketplace built around the supplier. Each showroom combines product collections with business and location details, giving buyers a fuller picture of who they are buying from.

Enquiries can cover a single product or a list for a larger order, with availability and terms discussed directly between buyer and supplier.

  • Browse by location
  • Product collections
  • Enquiry lists

LOGISTICS COMMUNICATION02 / 03

Bring freight and parcel providers into the conversation.

CargoChatter brings freight brokers, logistics service providers and express parcel delivery companies into the websites and apps where shipment enquiries begin. Customers can review a provider’s services and coverage, describe what needs moving and discuss routes, timing, handling and delivery options directly—from a single parcel to commercial freight.

The host platform selects which providers appear. A freight broker can discuss carrier coverage, a logistics operator can assess transport requirements, and a parcel company can clarify collection and delivery. Each provider handles its own availability, quotation and service.

  • Freight & parcel profiles
  • Shipment-specific chat
  • Provider-led quotations
Visit CargoChatter (opens in a new tab)
TRANSPORT VISIBILITY03 / 03
Illustration

Visibility across your transport network.

Loadgistic makes vehicle availability and routes visible across transport networks. Transporters decide what to share publicly and what to make available only to selected customers, while shippers and brokers can review capacity and keep track of providers they work with.

Agreed shipments can then be followed through private updates, giving the people involved a shared view of progress.

  • Vehicle capacity
  • Transporter networks
  • Private shipment updates

OUR PERSPECTIVE

That is the purpose behind our portfolio. We build for those everyday decisions, and continue developing the platforms as the relationships and needs around them change.
