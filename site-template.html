Discover what Africa makes.

AfricMade gives farms, makers and manufacturers a digital showroom for products, business details and location. Buyers can discover suppliers and send enquiries for a single item or a larger order, then agree availability and terms directly.

  • Location search
  • Product collections

02 / 03

Logistics conversations across platforms.

CargoChatter brings provider profiles and shipment conversations into websites and apps. Platforms choose which providers appear; buyers and sellers discuss shipments with them. Provider teams manage profiles, assign enquiries from participating sites and discuss shipment details and quotes in one inbox.

  • Website & app widgets
  • Provider selection
  • Team enquiry inbox

03 / 03

See capacity. Coordinate transport.

Loadgistic helps transporters manage vehicles and share capacity and routes publicly or with selected customers. Shippers and brokers can review available space and build their transporter network. Private updates keep the people involved in agreed shipments informed.

  • Fleet management
  • Capacity sharing
  • Private shipment updates
