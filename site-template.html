OUR PURPOSE

Trade depends
on information.

A company’s reach is shaped by how easily others can discover its products, understand its capabilities and make contact. In transport, the same principle applies to available vehicles and routes. Our platforms make that information accessible so businesses can recognise opportunities and act on them.

Our vision

African markets where businesses can earn visibility through what they offer, reach customers beyond their immediate networks and form lasting commercial relationships.

Our mission

We develop and operate platforms that improve access to markets, and continue to refine them as the needs of buyers, suppliers and transporters evolve.

Discover what Africa makes.

AfricMade brings African farms, manufacturers and independent makers into a marketplace built around the supplier. Each showroom combines product collections with business and location details, giving buyers a fuller picture of who they are buying from.

Enquiries can cover a single product or a list for a larger order, with availability and terms discussed directly between buyer and supplier.

  • Product collections

TRANSPORT VISIBILITY02 / 03
Illustration

Visibility across your transport network.

Loadgistic makes vehicle availability and routes visible across transport networks. Transporters decide what to share publicly and what to make available only to selected customers, while shippers and brokers can review capacity and keep track of providers they work with.

Agreed shipments can then be followed through private updates, giving the people involved a shared view of progress.

  • Vehicle capacity
  • Transporter networks
  • Private shipment updates
Visit Loadgistic (opens in a new tab)
LOGISTICS CHAT03 / 03

Connect customers directly with logistics providers.

CargoChatter embeds logistics provider profiles and live chat in websites and apps. Customers can explore services, discuss their shipment requirements and request quotes directly from a provider, for anything from a parcel to a full load.

Platform owners choose which providers appear, shaping a logistics offering around their customers while keeping the conversation within their own site or app.

  • Provider profiles
  • Live chat
  • Shipment quotes
